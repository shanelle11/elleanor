✦ My Poems ✦

📝💭📝

Welcome to my poetry corner! Click on any poem to read the full version~ ✨

⭐ 💖 ✨
♡★♡

"We read and write poetry because we are members of the human race. And the human race is filled with passion. And medicine, law, business, engineering, these are noble pursuits and necessary to sustain life. But poetry, beauty, romance, love, these are what we stay alive for."

— John Keating, "Dead Poets Society"
✿💫✿
🌸 💝 🌟

"In shades of pink and purple skies..."

In shades of pink and purple skies,
Where cotton candy clouds drift by,
I found a dream that never dies,
A world where hearts can truly fly.

Soft whispers of a gentle breeze,
Carry wishes through the air,
Dancing 'round the cherry trees,
Love blooms everywhere.

Your smile paints my world so bright,
Like stickers on a scrapbook page,
Every moment feels so right,
A story written, age to age.

In this pastel paradise we've made,
Where every color tells our tale,
Our love will never fade,
Like doodles on an endless trail.

"8-bit feelings in a digital world..."

8-bit feelings in a digital world,
Loading screens of memories unfurled,
Like a game we used to play,
Where pixels showed the way.

Dial-up tones and blinking lights,
Chat room conversations through the nights,
Emoticons with hearts so true,
Every :) reminded me of you.

Browser windows of the past,
Connections made to always last,
In this retro digital space,
I found my happy place.

Download love in megabytes,
Screen glow illuminating nights,
Though the world has moved ahead,
These pixel memories never dead.

"Every color tells a story bright..."

Every color tells a story bright,
Red for passion, burning light,
Orange sunsets, warm embrace,
Yellow sunshine on my face.

Green like meadows, fresh and new,
Blue like oceans, deep and true,
Purple dreams in twilight's glow,
Pink like flowers in a row.

Mix them all in joy's parade,
Rainbow thoughts that never fade,
Paint the world with happy hues,
Choose the colors you can't lose.

Life's a canvas, wild and free,
Splash it with creativity,
Every shade a different mood,
All combining, rainbow food for thought,
Lessons that the heart has taught.

"Upon a star I make my wish tonight..."

Upon a star I make my wish tonight,
Send it dancing through the sky,
Watch it sparkle, burning bright,
As it travels way up high.

Dreams are woven through the air,
Constellations of desire,
Magic floating everywhere,
Setting hopeful hearts on fire.

Catch a shooting star's embrace,
Hold the wonder in your hands,
Feel the stardust on your face,
Understand what the universe understands.

Every wish a tiny light,
Glowing in the cosmic sea,
Reaching through the endless night,
Whispering what we could be.

So I'll keep on wishing, dreaming true,
Under stars that know my name,
One day all my dreams come through,
And the universe plays my game.

"Float away on sugary dreams..."

Float away on sugary dreams,
Where nothing's quite the way it seems,
Cotton candy fills the sky,
Butterflies and birds fly by.

Marshmallow mountains, tall and white,
Lollipop trees in morning light,
Chocolate rivers, flowing sweet,
Gumdrop paths beneath our feet.

Sprinkle stars that twinkle bright,
Frosting falls on winter night,
Cookie crumb roads lead the way,
To a land where we can play.

In this whimsical parade,
All our worries seem to fade,
Life is sweet and sugar-spun,
Every day is filled with fun.

So close your eyes and take my hand,
Journey to this candy land,
Where imagination runs so free,
And we can be who we want to be.

"I draw hearts in my notebook margins..."

I draw hearts in my notebook margins,
Each one holds a thought of you,
Doodled stars and tiny gardens,
Sketching feelings, pure and true.

Arrow-pierced and intertwined,
Scribbled names in bubble font,
Every heart that I design,
Shows you everything I want.

Pink and purple, red and gold,
Markers bleeding through the page,
Stories that my heart has told,
Love poems written at this age.

Simple lines that say so much,
Curves that hold my deepest dreams,
Wishing for your gentle touch,
Nothing's ever as it seems.

These little hearts I draw each day,
Are messages I'm too shy to send,
But maybe you'll see them anyway,
And know my love will never end.

"This house is too quiet..."

This house is too quiet.

The kind of quiet that seeps into your bones,
settles into the walls,
makes a home out of the spaces you once filled.

I wake up before the sun again,
but there is no warmth beside me.
Just the cold stretch of sheets, undisturbed.

Even after all this time,
I still turn to tell you things.

I catch myself in the mirror sometimes.
the lines on my face deeper than they were before,
my hands more hesitant as they reach
for a teacup that once had a pair.

Dusty.
The polaroids on the dresser have started to drain off their colour.
Your smile...once so bright,
now barely holding onto the ink.

I should put them away.
Maybe even shred them away.

Why are you so pretty?

My hands refuse to let you go,
as if losing you in memory
would be the last betrayal.

"Won't you stay?"

I whisper it into the air,
into the empty seat at the table,
into the space beside me
where your pillow still rests untouched.

Love doesn't vanish.
It lingers in the places time cannot touch,
in the chair you always sat in,
in the old songs that still hum through the radio,
in the way the house still smells like your favorite flowers,
even though no fresh ones have been placed in years.

Gone were the warm breeze ruffling your hair,
and so were you, my darling, my Gracey.

I'll live only to die in you,
while you die but continue to live in me.

For as long as I am,
you'll be the happiest part of me,
and I'll be the saddest part of you.

The world tells me to move on.
To keep going, to let go,
but what do they know?

"Where love was, love will always be."

And where you were,
I will always remain.

They say everything has a time limit.
That even love has an expiration date.
But I think they are wrong.

Because even now, long after the clocks have stopped for you,
you are still my sun.
My Gracey.

And I,
I am simply the one left behind,
standing in the light you left behind,
waiting.

with love,
Ignatius

(For what greater punishment is there than life,
when the one you lived for is gone?)
